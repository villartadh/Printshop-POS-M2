# Native production workflow (foundation, not yet a feature)
Source parity: desktop src/shared/jobWorkflow.ts; native policy uses integer centavos and derives payment internally from a linked ledger. Existing paid and credit receipts remain authoritative. A pending job progresses to production, quality check, pickup and completed; quality rework returns to production and pickup can return to quality check. No skipping stages. An unpaid or partially paid job cannot complete; cancellation is available only before production for unlinked unpaid/zero-value jobs. A refunded/voided sale cannot be advanced; early statuses become cancelled after reversal, later historical statuses remain as recorded. Identical mutation replay must be handled by the future journal rather than duplicate transition.
