# Native quotation milestone (in progress)
Initial pure Java commercial snapshot foundation. A quotation has no tender, stock reservation, sale, receipt or fiscal effect. It preserves exact historical service, price profile, recipe and material revisions plus raw quantity/dimension inputs and cost/usage. Availability and pending stock are estimates at review, not promises. Insufficient availability does not prevent quoting; zero value or stock demand not representable in stock milliunits cannot be converted by the existing checkout policy. Conversion compares the fresh authoritative cash checkout commercial terms and rejects revisions/raw terms that differ; availability/tender can change and must independently pass the existing checkout gate. No database persistence, customer selection, credit conversion or transaction link yet. Do not expose in UI or claim exactly-once conversion until all are implemented and verified.
