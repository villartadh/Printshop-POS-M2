# Native receivable migration contract (not exposed yet)

The current v15 checkout, cash-payment constraint and immutable drawer/report sources assume the full price was paid. Do not implement credit by pretending unpaid cash was received, retroactively editing a cash receipt, or recording an ordinary cash-in as a collection.

## Phase 1 — exact review policy
- FULL_CASH accepts tender >= total; paid=total; change=tender-total; owed=0.
- PARTIAL_CASH requires active customer snapshot and 0<tender<total; paid=tender; change=0; owed=total-paid.
- UNPAID requires active customer, zero tender, paid=0; owed=total.
- A subsequent collection applies oldest open debt first (opened timestamp, stable ID), using immutable source allocation before/after amounts. Aggregate customer balances use BigInteger; each debt, collection and drawer amount is bounded exact centavos.
- A reviewed collection must match freshly calculated allocations, drawer balance and customer balance under the same writer transaction. Changed review is rejected, not silently reallocated. Persist exact request identity and resolve/replay in one transaction; never change an already applied request.
- Credit sale reversal must be a dedicated policy; legacy full cash refund path is prohibited even when later collections have settled the credit. No automatic stock return.

## Phase 2 — persistence migration and gates
Use additive explicitly typed credit source, or deliberately version the old cash checkout schema/constraints/fingerprint (with actual migrations and exact v15 fixture preservation). No fake full-cash receipt. A new applied credit sale must have the same authoritative service/material snapshot and idempotent inventory semantics as cash checkout. Atomically link the authenticated operator's own shift, customer revision, original selling price, initial cash paid and original debt; append stock movement once. For unpaid sales there is no cash effect; partial payment changes cash by initial paid only.

Collections must retain a unique stable pending identity (operator, shift, customer, amount, expected drawer and exact allocation list), reject future/foreign/stale/duplicate debt links and overpayment, and apply all allocations plus one cash effect or roll back all. Pending collections must block conflicting drawer/checkout/shift close activity. Historical customer archive must not erase balances; permit authorized settlement of an existing debt. Exactly-once apply/cancel/replay and recovery after logout/restart are mandatory.

Report gross sales by total of actual applied sale, not amount paid. Report collected cash independently: upfront cash + applied collections - cash refunds. Collection is not new sales revenue. Preserve original cash-only reports, net sale and shift reconciliation. Reconcile refund policy before exposing credit: no generic drawer adjustment or full cash compensation for credit sources. Track original balance and separately applied debt allocations; no unbacked customer balance edits.

Required host SQLite + actual Android tests: migrate populated v15 (including archived customer and named pending/full paid receipts); no invented debt; partial and zero upfront paid; exact stock and cash; duplicate/stale/foreign/overpay; competing shifts; cancellation and crash/rollback at every insertion; reopening exact identity; cumulative allocations and reports; closed shift restrictions; role isolation; installed screen input review and receipt details. Existing 28 installed markers stay mandatory. No production promotion or real financial activity. Current policy is host-tested only; schema/UI remain disabled.

## Phase 2a — isolated ledger SQL candidate
ReceivableJournal.createCandidate defines immutable prospective credit source rows with exact total/initial paid and original customer revision, plus one-pending-per-customer/shift collection records, exact FIFO per-debt allocation rows, and derived outstanding view. SQLite rejects wrong owner/shift/revision, duplicate identities, mutation/deletion, stale or cross-customer allocations, non-FIFO allocations, incomplete/overpayment resolution and conflicting pending customer credit. Cancellation retains audit without decrementing outstanding; archive permits settlement of existing debt.

This method deliberately has **no caller** in CatalogStore. It is exercised on disposable SQLite fixtures only. The candidate credit source is not yet linked to an authoritative applied stock/sale/cash transaction. The candidate collection resolution reduces the prototype outstanding but does not write a real drawer effect; therefore it must not be installed or exposed as a working collection API. The next change needs an additive durable credit checkout journal, exact actual source linkage and a unified typed drawer-effect chain, pending conflict/shift-close guards both directions, replay authentication, reporting, and migrations before enabling any financial operation. Do not infer SDK acceptance from host SQL checks.

## Phase 2b — unified cash chain candidate
CreditCashJournal.createCandidate, after ReceivableJournal.createCandidate, copies each prior drawer effect into an additive ordered chain with its exact original source and before/after values; the original audit and anchor tables remain immutable. It mirrors future legacy drawer effects into the same chain and derives the current drawer from the latest typed effect. Credit upfront payment appends exactly paid cash if positive; unpaid creates no cash effect; applied collection inserts exactly one effect via the same SQLite statement as the debt resolution. Faults roll back source/resolution and cash effect. Pending collections mutually exclude ordinary checkout links, drawer stage, new credit on the same shift and shift close; original guards remain. Separate credit reporting view returns gross, cash, created debt and collected debt separately. No collection is new revenue.

Still isolated: no CatalogStore migration caller, authenticated runtime API, authoritative credit service/material snapshot or stock consumption, durable credit checkout pending recovery, complete integrated ReportMath/ReportJournal or installed acceptance. Never expose partial/unpaid or collections before these are complete. The cash prototype tests synthetic source rows, not actual sales.

## Phase 2c — authoritative review and stock journal candidate
CreditCheckoutPlan derives a separate credit fingerprint from existing exact pure checkout pricing/material snapshot plus customer identity/revision and real paid mode/amount. Its internal exact-total pricing calculation is never returned or persisted as a payment; the public result retains actual tender and original owed. The old full-cash underpayment rule remains unchanged.

CreditCheckoutJournal.createCandidate (after both earlier candidates) adds an immutable pending credit request and exact recipe/stock snapshot. Apply requires current service, price tier, recipe revision, complete material list, unchanged exact stock revision/balance and authenticated open shift, then one SQLite statement inserts all stock movements, applied source and upfront cash effect via triggers. Fault rolls back all three. Source insertion is fenced to the exact applied credit request, so no unbacked standalone debt can be created in the combined candidate. Cancel has no sale effects, and pending credit blocks shift close and conflicting checkout/drawer/collection. Current customer revision must still be active at apply through the source guard. Historical stock effects immutable.

No migration caller yet. The next step must persist full raw inputs and reconstruct the exact CreditCheckoutPlan on stage/apply using actual CatalogStore read snapshots (including dimensions, pages/copies, basis and price), implement authenticated durable stage/review/read/replay/cancel APIs (including same fingerprint and customer identity), and integrate the schema/reporting in v16. SQL alone does not authenticate the caller or reconstruct all inputs. No financial UI exposed or production acceptance.

## Phase 2d — v16 schema and authenticated training credit API
CatalogStore now calls all three journals on create and upgrade to v16. Credit checkout stores immutable raw inputs separately and reconstructs the authoritative pricing/material plan inside the writer transaction on stage/apply. Exact fingerprint and operator/shift ownership govern read, pending discovery, apply/cancel and replay. Only synthetic fixture methods are available; no credit button or collection caller is enabled. Legacy owner sales reports deliberately fail closed after any credit source until integrated credit-inclusive report math is ready. Existing full-cash receipt/refund sources remain separate.

Host SQLite validates schema and raw-input immutability; actual Android instrumentation cases are authored but not executed. Pending restart, populated v15 customer-linked migration, complete report integration, authenticated collection API, credit UI and mandatory installed/platform gates remain acceptance work.

## Phase 2e — authenticated collection API (v16 unchanged)
CatalogStore exposes synthetic-fixture-only collection prepare/stage/read/pending/apply/cancel methods and exact outstanding for authenticated operators on their own open shifts. Archived customers can settle debt. Stage recomputes the entire review under the writer transaction; apply checks the persisted FIFO allocation and exact drawer against current data. The replay fingerprint binds durable ID, customer/revision, shift/operator, cash amounts and ordered allocation lines; aggregate outstanding is informational at review and not stored as a historical aggregate. Resolved replay cannot create another cash effect. The existing SQL triggers maintain atomic debt and cash effects; collection has no stock or gross sales effect.

The combined checkout/collection host fixture now uses actual applied checkout sources to test archived debt settlement and cash/stock separation. New installed acceptance assertions are authored, not run. Legacy reports still fail closed on credit; no cashier credit/collection screens or production approval.
